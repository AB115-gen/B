for Saya

Dearest Saya,

For your birthday the sky has brought out its softest clouds, the garden has put on its best green, and Dllo has, naturally, claimed the sunniest spot on the sill. He carried this letter in himself, and would like that noted.

May the year ahead be as gentle as a morning cloud, as green as an ivied wall, and quietly full of purrs. You make the world a kinder, lovelier place, and everyone who knows you is the luckier for it.

Happy birthday.

with love,your friend

make a wish

Five candles. Blow them out, then whisper a wish to the clouds.

press to blow, or tap a flame

Sent. Only the clouds heard it, and they never tell.

play with Dllo

Yarn is falling from the clouds. Move Dllo underneath and help him catch as much as he can in thirty seconds.

caught 030s

Dllo is waiting.

Slide your finger, or use the arrow keys, to move him under the yarn. Gold yarn counts for three.

September the twentieth, every year, and this one most of all.
